# Sample prompts

Built-in presets in **Settings → Prompt preset**:

## Scene describe
Describe what I am looking at in 2 sentences. Be specific about objects and any visible text.

## Navigation
Identify street signs, storefront names, and suggest which direction I should turn if relevant.

## Read text (accessibility)
Read any visible text in a clear, spoken-friendly way: menus, labels, and signs.

## Safety check
Flag hazards in my field of view: obstacles, curbs, vehicles, or uneven ground.

## Shopping
Identify products on shelves and any visible price tags you can read.

## Scene context
How many people are in view and describe the general scene context. Do not identify individuals.

---

**Tips**

- Use **Analyze now** for an immediate high-detail snapshot.
- Lower the analysis interval only when plugged in; streaming uses Bluetooth bandwidth.
- Gemini Flash is the default for cost and free-tier access; switch to OpenAI in Settings if needed.

## Chat prompt ideas

Use these in **Chat** (optionally with **Attach current frame**):

- “What am I looking at? Be concise.”
- “Read any visible text and summarize it.”
- “What’s the safest path forward from what you can see?”
- “List objects in view and any warnings I should know.”
